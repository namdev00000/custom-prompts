# Constructive Feedback and Course Correction

**Goal:** Get objective, judgment-free corrections when learning a new skill.

## The Prompt
> "I am learning [Topic/Skill] and will provide my current understanding or work. Please highlight any mistakes I make or point out if I am heading in the wrong direction. Keep the focus on curious improvement and excellence over perfectionism, without any judgment. Use simple language to explain where I went wrong and how to fix it."
